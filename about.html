Who We Are

BAM Studios is an independent software studio based in Pakistan. We design and build secure, Halal SaaS products — for the Muslim Ummah and for developers worldwide who want to build with principle.

Our Vision

To become a trusted name in Halal technology — creating products that serve both Deen and Dunya, without compromise.

Our Mission

Build tools that make it easy to create secure, high-quality apps — without Riba, haram ads, or haram content — while keeping security and quality first, always.

Our Non-Negotiables

Every product we ship follows these four principles — without exception:

  1. Every legal and financial decision includes Parent/Guardian involvement.
  2. No Riba (interest) based loans — and no haram ads, ever.
  3. No project goes live without proper security (Row Level Security).
  4. Quality and Halal earning always come first.
