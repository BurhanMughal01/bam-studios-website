01 Our Promise

BAM Studios respects your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

We do not sell your data. We do not show haram ads. We do not spy on you.

02 What We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of information needed to run our services:

  • Account information — Email address and password (hashed) when you sign up.
  • Usage data — Anonymous analytics (page views, errors). No personal tracking.
  • Product data — Content you create (APK projects, chat history in BAM Coder).
  • Contact form data — Name, email, and message when you contact us.

03 What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not track you across other websites.
  • We do not use invasive third-party trackers (no Facebook Pixel, no tracking Google Analytics).
  • We do not access your device's contacts, photos, or files without permission.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone — ever.

04 How We Use Your Information

  • To provide our services (login, build projects, save data).
  • To send essential emails (welcome, builds, password reset).
  • To improve our products based on aggregated usage patterns.
  • To respond to your messages.

05 How We Protect Your Data

  • Row Level Security (RLS) on every database table — your data is visible only to you.
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) on all pages and APIs.
  • Hashed passwords — we never see your actual password.
  • Regular security reviews and principle-based engineering.

06 Third-Party Services

We use these trusted services to run our products:

  • Supabase — Authentication and database.
  • Cloudflare — Hosting, security, and API delivery.
  • Resend — Transactional email delivery.
  • Groq — AI inference for BAM Coder (no training on your data).
  • GitHub — Source code hosting and build systems.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy, and we choose them for their strong security practices.

07 Your Rights

  • Access — Request a copy of your data.
  • Correction — Update or correct your information.
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Portability — Export your data at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at bamstudio.Dev@gmail.com.

08 Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users aged 13 and above. If you are under 18, we require Parent/Guardian consent before creating an account — this is one of our non-negotiable principles.

09 Cookies

We use only essential cookies needed for authentication and security. No tracking cookies. No advertising cookies.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced on our website or via email. Continued use of our services after changes means you accept the updated policy.
